The only third-party sheet music included in Better Music Sheet is the
"Try a sample" score, *Ode to Joy* by Ludwig van Beethoven. We have the right to
use it:

- **Composition:** Beethoven died in 1827, so the work is in the public domain
  worldwide.
- **Engraving:** the score is from the Mutopia Project (www.MutopiaProject.org,
  reference Mutopia-2009/08/05-528), typeset with LilyPond by Peter Chubb, who
  placed it in the public domain. The footer of the attached PDF states:
  "This sheet music has been placed in the public domain by the typesetter" and
  links to http://creativecommons.org/licenses/publicdomain.
- Mutopia describes its scores as "Free to download, with the freedom to
  distribute, modify and perform."
- The app contains no lyrics and no other third-party scores. Screenshots and
  previews show only this score and the user's own uploads.

All other sheets in the app are uploaded by users for their own private use.
They are not shared, published or visible to other users. Our Terms require
users to upload only music they have the right to use.
