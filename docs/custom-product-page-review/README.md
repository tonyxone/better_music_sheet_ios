# Custom product page proposal: Guided Piano Practice

The four iPhone screenshots and the promotional text were uploaded to the [Guided Piano Practice custom product page draft](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/apps/6814721766/distribution/productpages/cc10ccc2-012d-4fe3-80eb-b1935285c771). The page has not been added or submitted for App Review.

## Suggested setup

- Reference name: `Guided Piano Practice`
- Audience: people who want to practise a piano piece from a PDF or photo
- Locale: English (U.S.)
- Promotional text (161 characters):

> Turn a PDF or photo of sheet music into guided piano practice. Read note names on the score, follow falling notes and lit keys, and slow down for the hard parts.
